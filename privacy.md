# Privacy Policy — Bill Forecaster

*Last updated: 26 November 2026*

## The short version

**Bill Forecaster collects nothing.** There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Your
balance, your bills, your income and your payment history are kept on your device and, as a
backup, in **your own** iCloud Drive. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Nobody, including the developer, can see your financial information. There is no mechanism by
which we could, because the app never sends anything to us.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored on your device:

- The account balance you type, and the date you say it was true
- The bills and income you set up, including amounts, dates and categories
- Records of what you have marked as paid, pre-paid or skipped
- Your settings, such as your safety floor and forecast range

This data lives in the app's own private storage, protected by your device's security.

## Automatic backups

Once a day, the app saves a backup copy of your data and keeps the last seven. If you are signed
in to iCloud, these copies go to a **Bill Forecaster** folder in your own iCloud Drive, where you
can see them in the Files app. That is so your data survives a lost or replaced phone. If iCloud
is not available, the copies stay inside the app on your device instead.

Those copies are stored in your iCloud account, under your Apple Account, and are protected the
way Apple protects everything else in your iCloud Drive. The developer has no access to your
iCloud account and never receives them.

Deleting the app removes the data on your device, but **not** the backups in iCloud Drive. You can
delete those yourself in the Files app, under iCloud Drive › Bill Forecaster.

## Backups and spreadsheets you export

You can also save a backup file, or export a spreadsheet of your upcoming bills, by hand. **You**
choose where those go: iCloud Drive, Files, or anywhere else you send them. Once exported, those
files are yours and are governed by wherever you put them, not by this app.

## Face ID lock

You can choose to have the app ask for Face ID (or your passcode) before it opens. The check is
done by iOS itself. The app is only told whether it succeeded; it never sees or stores your face
data or your passcode.

## Notifications

The app can send you notifications: a warning that your balance is heading below your safety
floor, and reminders before a bill is due. These are scheduled **on your device** by the app
itself. No notification is sent from a server, and no information about them leaves your phone.

## The home screen widget

The widget reads a small summary of your forecast from a shared container on your device. That
summary stays on the device.

## Tracking and advertising

There is none. Bill Forecaster does not track you, does not contain advertising, does not include
any analytics or crash-reporting service, and shares nothing with any third party. It has no
third-party code in it at all.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, of any age.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
