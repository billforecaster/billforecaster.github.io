# Bill Forecaster — Support

**Bill Forecaster** projects your account forward day by day and tells you when you run low,
before you get there.

## Getting help

Email **cowjuicer@gmail.com**. Please include your iOS version and what you were doing when the
problem happened — that is usually enough to identify it.

## Common questions

### My balance looks wrong

The balance is worked out from the figure you last typed, plus everything you have marked as paid
or skipped since. If it has drifted, the fastest fix is to open **Settings**, enter what your bank
actually says, and set the date to today. Everything recorded after that date is applied on top.

### A bill I paid is still showing

Marking a bill paid is what tells the forecast the money has moved. Swipe the occurrence in the
forecast list and reconcile it — at the projected amount, or a corrected one if it came out
different.

### I entered a bill and it repeats on the wrong day

An event repeats on the day it starts. If the repeat looks wrong, open the event and check the
**Starting** date and the recurrence rows underneath it — they should agree.

### Will my data sync between devices?

Not currently. Your data lives on one device. To move it to another, restore a backup there (see
below).

### How do I back up?

You don't have to. The app saves a backup once a day and keeps the last seven. If you are signed
in to iCloud, they are in the Files app under **iCloud Drive › Bill Forecaster**.

You can also save one by hand any time with **Settings → Save a backup**. To get your data back,
or to move it to a new phone, use **Settings → Restore from a backup** and pick a file.

### Can I lock the app?

Yes. Switch on **Require Face ID** in Settings, under Privacy. The app will then ask for Face ID,
or your passcode, each time you open it.

### How do I get reminded about a bill?

Open the bill, and switch on **Remind me** under Reminder. You can choose how many days ahead.
Reminders arrive at 9am, and only for bills you have not already reconciled.

## Privacy

Bill Forecaster collects nothing. No account, no server, no analytics. See the
[privacy policy](privacy.md).
